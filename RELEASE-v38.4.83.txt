Parknacross Weather v38.4.83
Released: 24 September 2026

- Replaced the falsely precise Poulshone water-temperature blend with a clearly
  labelled range spanning the local coastal model and fresh M2 observation.
- Clarified that neither temperature source is measured at Poulshone and added
  an explicit confidence statement.
- Made the Dashboard render current conditions as soon as the live reading
  arrives, without waiting for slower charts and archive summaries.
- Fixed offline matching for versioned same-origin assets on unvisited pages.
- Centralised documented rainfall corrections in data-corrections.js.
- Renamed “What to wear today” to “What to wear now” and clarified that it uses
  the latest observation rather than a full-day forecast.
- Renamed Storm Mode to “Significant weather review” and clearly identified its
  thresholds as site-defined indicators rather than official warnings.
- Grouped the More menu into Explore, Reports, and Site & app sections.
- Added archive date coverage to Downloads and made the System Status check time
  a live announced status.
- Moved the optional sky-photo display name into a collapsed disclosure.
- Added calm-wind percentage and threshold wording to wind-rose explanations.
- Expanded privacy retention/removal wording and regression checks.
- Standardised release query strings and advanced the PWA cache.
