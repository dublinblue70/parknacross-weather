PARKNACROSS WEATHER v38.4.116 — COMPLETE SENSOR CSV EXPORTS

Root-cause correction
- Replaces the single unbounded oldest-first D1 export query with explicit keyset pagination.
- Every export page advances by the unique observation epoch until the newest saved record is included.
- This prevents 30, 90 and 365-day files from stopping among older, pre-sensor observations.
- The same complete retrieval is used for 7-day files.

Clearer CSV data
- Adds observation_time_ireland as a readable local timestamp.
- Adds lightning_time_ireland alongside the retained machine-readable lightning_time_epoch.
- Lightning time is included only for rows that report a strike.
- The download confirmation reports how many rows contain soil data and lightning data.
- Soil and lightning readings remain blank before their sensors were installed; no historical values are invented.

Release and compatibility
- Website and PWA cache advanced to v38.4.116.
- Worker advanced to v38.4.72; weather ingestion and stored observations remain unchanged.
