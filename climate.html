How to read the fingerprint

Days run left to right; months run top to bottom. Each coloured segment grows with its reading: temperature high −5 to 30°C, rainfall 0 to 30 mm, peak gust 0 to 100 km/h, solar peak 0 to 1,000 W/m². Values outside these display ranges use the smallest or largest segment; the details show the actual reading. A small coloured segment includes a recorded zero; a dotted outline means not recorded.

Solar peak is the strongest recorded sunlight intensity, not sunshine duration or total solar energy. Today is incomplete; gaps in saved observations can affect any day’s totals and extremes. These fixed visual scales are not climate averages or warning thresholds. Use arrow keys to move between dates.
