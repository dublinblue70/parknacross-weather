How to read the data

Observed locally

Parknacross station

Direct WS90/GW3001 observations. Always check the observation time.

Official

Public authority data

Met Éireann, EPA or Marine Institute information, shown separately from local readings.

Modelled / predicted

Estimates and tides

Useful context, but not a measurement made at Parknacross or Poulshone.

Partial archive

Incomplete period

A partial result is never presented as a complete month or selected range.
