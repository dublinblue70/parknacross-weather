PARKNACROSS WEATHER v38.4.67
Released: 24 September 2026

WEATHER INTELLIGENCE LAB
- Added one coherent Weather Lab destination to the More menu.
- Coastal timeline combines current Parknacross observations, upcoming Arklow
  tide events, the available official point forecast and marine warning state.
- Storm Mode analyses the latest 24 hours for pressure change, peak gust,
  peak rain rate and available WH57 lightning-counter change.
- Ask the Archive answers common plain-language questions from saved daily data.
- Recent automatically identified weather events are presented as stories.
- Today’s sky can be downloaded with a branded weather overlay when browser
  image-security rules permit it.
- Forecast verification shows completed forecast-versus-observation results.
- Microclimate comparison presents Parknacross and Johnstown Castle context.
- Source/confidence explanations distinguish local, official, modelled and
  partial-archive information.
- Public JSON/CSV links, a simple embed example and official water-safety links
  are provided.

SAFETY AND ACCURACY
- The coastal timeline explicitly does not declare swimming conditions safe.
- Official warnings and local signs remain authoritative.
- No core weather calculations, archive aggregation, wind rose or lightning
  logic was changed.
