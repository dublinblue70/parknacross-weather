Visible satellite imagery uses reflected sunlight and is clearest in daylight; natural-colour and enhanced products may look different from an ordinary photograph. The satellite scans about every 15 minutes. Image times and availability depend on the provider. Satellite imagery shows cloud patterns, not rainfall measured at Parknacross.

Why radar can differ from the station

Radar estimates precipitation over an area. The figures on the Parknacross dashboard are measured locally by the WS90, so differences are normal.
