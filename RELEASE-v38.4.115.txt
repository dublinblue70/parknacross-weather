PARKNACROSS WEATHER v38.4.115 — FRESH SOIL & LIGHTNING CSV EXPORTS

CSV corrections
- Downloads now request a fresh database snapshot instead of accepting a potentially six-hour-old edge-cached export.
- The Worker bypasses its CSV edge cache when fresh=1 is requested by the Downloads page.
- Normal direct exports now expire after five minutes rather than six hours.
- The Downloads page validates that all soil and lightning columns exist before saving the file.
- Successful downloads explicitly confirm that weather, lightning and soil columns are included.
- Missing historical sensor readings remain blank; they are never converted into false zero measurements.

Included sensor columns
- lightning_distance_km
- lightning_strikes
- lightning_time_epoch
- soil_channel
- soil_moisture_pct
- soil_temperature_c
- soil_ec_us_cm

Release and compatibility
- Website and PWA cache advanced to v38.4.115.
- Worker advanced to v38.4.71 with ingestion and archive storage behaviour preserved.
