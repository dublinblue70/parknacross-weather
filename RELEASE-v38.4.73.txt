Parknacross Weather v38.4.73
Released: 24 September 2026

Final review corrections and usability improvements

- Corrected Storm Mode to read the real WH57 lightning_strikes archive field.
- Excluded counter resets and long archive gaps from Storm Mode lightning totals.
- Removed Forecast verification from the Dashboard and Climate page, including its unused requests.
- Replaced the Dashboard card with a useful saved-observation archive summary.
- Replaced the unfinished Sky camera journey with Today’s sky and the latest manually uploaded photograph.
- Added supported archive-question shortcuts and useful new archive queries.
- Added refresh controls and updated times to Graphs and Weather Lab.
- Renamed technical Storm Mode triggers as storm thresholds.
- Clarified the lightning chart axis as detected events.
- Increased System Status request tolerance and now distinguishes a slow response from an outage.

Weather measurements, chart calculations outside the documented corrections, archive storage and alert thresholds are unchanged.
