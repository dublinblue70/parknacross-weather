About the Parknacross station

Parknacross Weather is a privately operated personal weather station in the Ardamine area of north County Wexford. The exact residential location is intentionally not published.

The setup

The outdoor sensor is an Ecowitt WS90 ultrasonic weather array, paired with an Ecowitt GW3001 gateway. Together they measure the local conditions that appear on the dashboard, including temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, rainfall, pressure, solar radiation and UV.

The station is intended as a local hobby and community weather resource. It is useful for seeing what is actually happening in Parknacross, especially where coastal conditions can differ from larger official weather stations further inland.

Important note

Personal observations, not official warnings

Parknacross Weather is not an official forecasting or warning service. For safety-critical decisions, weather warnings and official forecasts, always use Met Éireann. The dashboard may occasionally be delayed by internet, power, gateway or cloud-service interruptions.
