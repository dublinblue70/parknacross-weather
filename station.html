About the Parknacross station

Parknacross Weather is an independently operated personal weather station in the Ardamine area of north County Wexford, Ireland. It provides local observations for a coastal location where conditions can vary noticeably from larger inland stations. The exact residential position is intentionally not published.

Station specification

An Ecowitt WS90 ultrasonic outdoor array supplies the primary weather observations, paired with an Ecowitt GW3001 gateway. The station measures temperature, humidity, wind, rainfall, pressure, solar radiation and UV, with observations passed to the Parknacross Weather cloud archive and dashboard.

The WS90 is roof mounted at a residential coastal site. Its observations describe the local Parknacross microclimate and are not intended to replicate an official World Meteorological Organization (WMO) reference station.

The dashboard updates about once a minute, while historical readings are saved every five minutes to build a reliable long-term record.
