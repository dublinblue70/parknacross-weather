About the Parknacross station

Parknacross Weather is an independently operated personal weather station in the Ardamine area of north County Wexford, Ireland. It provides local observations for a coastal location where conditions can vary noticeably from larger inland stations. The exact residential position is intentionally not published.

Full station specification

Equipment & location

Parknacross Weather uses an Ecowitt WS90 ultrasonic outdoor array paired with an Ecowitt GW3001 gateway. The WS90 is roof-mounted at a residential coastal location in Ardamine and records the local Parknacross microclimate.

As a residential installation, the site is not intended to replicate an official World Meteorological Organization (WMO) reference station.

Reading the weather

Understanding these observations

A little context helps when comparing Parknacross with another station.

A residential coastal setting

The array is mounted on a residential roof. Nearby buildings, the roof and shelter can influence wind and temperature. These observations describe this particular location; they are not an official reference-station record.

Rain detected by impact

The WS90 estimates rainfall using a piezoelectric sensor rather than a tipping bucket. Treat very small amounts with care when comparing rain totals with another gauge.

Lightning distance is approximate

The detector reports nearby lightning with an estimated range up to about 40 km. A zero count means no strikes were reported by this detector, rather than proof that no lightning occurred.

Soil readings are very local

The soil sensor describes its own planting location. Moisture and root-zone temperature can differ elsewhere in the garden. Conductivity describes electrical conductivity; it is not a direct measure of plant nutrition.

Background alerts, thresholds & quiet hours

Checking background delivery…

Quiet hours suppress notifications; missed alerts are not queued for later. A matching start and end mutes the whole day. Rain-start alerts are limited to once in three hours, gust alerts to once in two hours, low-temperature alerts to once in six hours, heavy-rain alerts to once an hour, and lightning alerts to once in 15 minutes.

Recent background alerts sent

These are local station notifications, not official weather warnings. Met Éireann remains the source for official warnings and safety-critical decisions.

Important

Personal observations

Parknacross Weather is not an official forecasting or warning service. For safety-critical decisions and official weather warnings, always use Met Éireann. Local observations may occasionally be delayed by internet, power, gateway or cloud-service interruptions.
