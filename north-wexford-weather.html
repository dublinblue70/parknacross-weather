North Wexford weather

Parknacross Weather is a local observation site for the North Wexford coast. It is designed to add hyperlocal detail to regional forecasts by showing what the weather is actually doing at the station.

North Wexford now

The dashboard publishes live station readings and short-term trends for temperature, wind, rainfall, pressure, humidity, solar radiation and UV.

Regional context

The site includes rain radar, Met Éireann warnings and comparison with official Johnstown Castle observations to put Parknacross readings into a wider Wexford context.

Rainfall and climate

Explore rainfall, climate context, monthly reports and weather records as the archive grows.

Open weather archive

Historical station observations can be viewed on the history page or exported as CSV from the data archive for personal analysis.
