PARKNACROSS WEATHER v38.4.113 — CORRECT LUNAR PHASE ORDER

Lunar presentation corrections
- Uses the standard eight-phase order: New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, Waning Crescent.
- Narrows the Full Moon and other principal-phase windows so a 97% illuminated, waning Moon is correctly shown as Waning Gibbous.
- Changes “Next major phase” to “Next milestone” to make clear that the countdown refers to the next principal lunar event, not the next intermediate description.
- Suppresses redundant waxing/waning text beside the exact New Moon and Full Moon labels.
- Adds an automated sequence test covering all eight phases and the September 2026 post-full case.
