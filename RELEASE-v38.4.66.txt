PARKNACROSS WEATHER v38.4.66
Released: 24 September 2026

SEA AND SWIM CONDITIONS
- Reframed the existing Coastal page as Sea & Swim Conditions.
- Added a concise factual overview combining the latest Parknacross air
  temperature, wind direction/speed, coastal exposure and rain rate.
- Includes the existing modelled local sea temperature, observed offshore M2
  reference, Arklow tide prediction and relevant official marine warning.
- Clearly states that the page describes conditions and does not declare the
  sea safe.
- Renamed the More-menu destination consistently across the website.

SHAREABLE MONTHLY REPORTS
- Added a branded 1200×630 monthly weather report card.
- Uses the selected archived month and its actual mean temperature, high/low,
  rainfall, gust and narrative summary.
- Shares through supported mobile share sheets or downloads a PNG elsewhere.
- Added a print-friendly monthly report layout.

ALREADY RETAINED
- The Dashboard share card continues to provide a branded current-day image.
- History retains weather-event stories, the archive calendar and event diary.
- Weather calculations, archive aggregation, wind roses and lightning logic
  are unchanged.
