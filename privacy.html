Privacy, simply explained

Your privacy at Parknacross Weather

You do not need an account to use this website. We do not sell personal information, serve personalised advertising or ask for your precise location.

The short version

Mostly weather, very little personal data

  • View all weather pages without signing in.
  • Basic anonymous visit statistics help show which pages are useful.
  • App preferences stay in your browser.
  • You control notifications and can turn them off at any time.

What is recorded?

Cloudflare Web Analytics supplies aggregated information such as page views, broad device type and the page that referred you. It is used to understand site performance—not to build an advertising profile.

What stays on your device?

Installation preferences, notification choices and an anonymous sky-photo identifier may be saved in your browser. You can remove these by clearing the website’s stored data.

Photo likes

An anonymous identifier prevents repeated likes on one photograph. Adding a display name is optional. If supplied, the name is visible only to the photo administrator.

Notifications

The website asks for notification permission only after you choose to enable alerts. Permission can be changed or withdrawn in your browser or phone settings.

Questions or removal requests

Email info@parknacrossweather.ie if you have a privacy question or want a saved display name or interaction removed. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Technical details and service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare provides the weather-data service, archive and aggregated analytics. Met Éireann and the Marine Institute provide some official weather and marine information. Sky photographs uploaded by the station administrator may be retained as part of the local weather archive.
