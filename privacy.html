Your privacy

Parknacross Weather privacy information

A plain-language explanation of the limited information used to operate the website and installed app.

Weather visitors

You can view the live weather, charts and archive without creating an account or providing your name, email address or precise location.

Website analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregated information such as page views, broad device type and referring page. Parknacross Weather does not use this information to build personal advertising profiles.

Local app settings

The website may store installation preferences, notification choices and an anonymous Sky Photo visitor identifier in your browser. These settings remain associated with that browser unless you clear its website data.

Sky Photo likes

A random anonymous browser identifier is used to prevent repeated likes on the same photo. If you voluntarily save a display name, it is associated with that interaction. Do not enter private or sensitive information as a display name.

Notifications

Notification permission is requested only when you choose to enable weather notifications. You can change or withdraw permission in your browser or device settings.

Photographs

Sky photographs are uploaded by the station administrator and may be retained as part of the local weather archive. Images should not deliberately identify people or neighbouring private property.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Cloudflare provides the weather-data service, archive and privacy-focused analytics. External official information may be obtained from Met Éireann and the Marine Institute.

Contact and removal requests

For a privacy question or to request removal of a saved display name or interaction, email info@parknacrossweather.ie. Include enough information to identify the item without sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Updates to this notice

This notice was last updated on 23 September 2026. It will be updated if the website begins collecting materially different information or introduces store-distributed applications.
