Parknacross Weather v38.4.140
Mobile sound level and morning birdsong

Changes to the optional Weather Window soundscape:
- Increase the wind and rain signal gain and set the initial volume slider to 85% to make playback more audible on phone speakers.
- Add a compressor to control peaks when wind, rain and chirps overlap.
- Resume Web Audio directly in the Play tap, before generating the noise/chirp buffers, to better match iOS Safari and mobile Chrome audio policies.
- Add a short locally synthesized birdlike chirp layer from 05:00 to 12:00 Europe/Dublin time. No real bird recording or external audio is used.
- Keep tap-to-play, volume control and automatic pause while the page is hidden.
- Keep the “Illustrated from local readings” caption removed.
- Cache-bust dashboard assets and update the service-worker cache to v38.4.140.

Existing dashboard readings, calculations, charts, alerts, forecasts and Worker APIs are unchanged. The optional sound component remains isolated from the established rendering.
