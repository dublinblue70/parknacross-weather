How the comparison works

Same time, same location. The two forecasts cover precisely the same 12-hour period (11 or 13 hours across a clock change), for the Ardamine area. Predictions are saved before the period starts; historical forecasts are never reconstructed after the event.

Independent measurement. The station's dedicated traditional rain gauge supplies the observation. Piezo-only estimates are not used. Missing, interrupted or reset gauge records are excluded instead of being counted as zero.

Fair scoring. Mean absolute error is the average difference, in millimetres, between forecast and observation; smaller is better. The website only identifies a leading model after at least 10 comparable periods, and does not claim a winner for near-ties. This is a local track record, not a guarantee of future accuracy.
